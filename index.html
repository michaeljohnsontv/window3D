Window3D

This page turns your monitor into a window: it tracks your face with the webcam and redraws the scene from where your eyes are, every frame.

The webcam image is processed locally in this browser. No video leaves your computer. (The tracking code and face model are downloaded from public CDNs.)

For best results: press F for fullscreen, set your screen’s visible width and height in the settings panel, and wear red (left) / cyan (right) glasses for the stereo modes.
